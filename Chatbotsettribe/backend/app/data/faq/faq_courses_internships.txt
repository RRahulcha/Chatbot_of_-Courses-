INTERNSHIP QUESTIONS

QUESTION:
Does SETTribe provide internships?

ANSWER:
Yes. SETTribe provides internship opportunities in technology-related domains depending on current availability.

QUESTION:
What internship domains are available?

ANSWER:
Internship opportunities may be available in areas such as software development, web development, Python, data analytics, data science, artificial intelligence, cyber security, and other technology domains depending on current opportunities.

QUESTION:
What happens during an internship?

ANSWER:
Interns may participate in practical project work, technical activities, development tasks, problem solving, documentation, mentoring, and professional activities depending on the internship program.

QUESTION:
Are projects included in internships?

ANSWER:
Project work may be part of an internship. The exact project and responsibilities depend on the specific internship opportunity.

QUESTION:
How long is an internship?

ANSWER:
Internship duration varies according to the specific program. The chatbot should retrieve the exact duration from the current internship information.

QUESTION:
Who can apply for an internship?

ANSWER:
Eligibility depends on the individual internship opportunity. Students, graduates, and learners may be eligible according to the applicable requirements.

QUESTION:
Is there mentorship during an internship?

ANSWER:
Mentorship or technical guidance may be provided depending on the internship structure.

QUESTION:
Will I receive a certificate after the internship?

ANSWER:
Certificate availability and conditions depend on the specific internship program. The chatbot should not guarantee a certificate unless the relevant official information confirms it.

QUESTION:
Is an internship useful for a fresher?

ANSWER:
An internship can help a fresher gain practical experience, develop technical skills, work on projects, strengthen a portfolio, and understand professional workflows.

QUESTION:
Does an internship provide industry exposure?

ANSWER:
Internships are designed to provide practical and industry-oriented exposure through project work, professional workflows, collaboration, technical problem solving, and mentorship where applicable.

QUESTION:
Does SETTribe provide job placement after an internship?

ANSWER:
An internship should not be presented as a guaranteed job or placement unless an official program explicitly states such a guarantee.

STEP AND CAREER PREPARATION

QUESTION:
What is STEP?

ANSWER:
STEP stands for Skill Transition & Employability Program and focuses on helping learners develop technical skills, apply them through practical projects, and prepare for professional careers.

QUESTION:
Does STEP include practical projects?

ANSWER:
Yes. STEP emphasizes practical learning through projects, case studies, and industry-oriented activities depending on the selected program.

QUESTION:
Does STEP provide industry exposure?

ANSWER:
STEP emphasizes industry-oriented learning and practical exposure through projects, mentorship, case studies, and professional practices depending on the program.

QUESTION:
Does STEP provide resume support?

ANSWER:
Resume development and career preparation are part of the STEP employability approach.

QUESTION:
Does STEP provide LinkedIn support?

ANSWER:
LinkedIn guidance may be included as part of professional and career preparation.

QUESTION:
Does STEP provide interview preparation?

ANSWER:
Yes. Interview preparation can include technical interview preparation, mock interviews, HR preparation, aptitude preparation, communication skills, and professional development.

QUESTION:
Does STEP provide mentorship?

ANSWER:
Mentorship is part of STEP's learning and career-oriented approach, with the exact mentoring structure depending on the program.

QUESTION:
Does STEP guarantee a job?

ANSWER:
The chatbot must not claim guaranteed employment or placement unless explicitly confirmed by current official program information.
