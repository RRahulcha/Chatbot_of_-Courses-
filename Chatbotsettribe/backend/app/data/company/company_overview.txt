STEP BY SETTRIBE - PROGRAM OVERVIEW

PROGRAM NAME:
STEP

FULL FORM:
Skill Transition & Employability Program

ORGANIZATION:
SETTribe

TAGLINE / APPROACH:
From College to Corporate

OVERVIEW:
STEP is a Skill Transition & Employability Program designed to help students and professionals develop industry-relevant technical skills, apply those skills through practical projects, and prepare for professional careers.

STEP follows a learning approach based on:

LEARN → APPLY → TRANSITION

LEARN:
Participants develop technical and professional skills through structured, industry-oriented learning.

APPLY:
Participants apply their learning through practical assignments, projects, case studies, and industry-oriented activities.

TRANSITION:
Participants prepare for the transition from education to employment through career preparation, mentorship, interview preparation, professional development, and other employability activities.

WHO STEP IS FOR:
STEP is designed primarily for:

* College students
* Recent graduates
* Early-career learners
* Professionals looking to develop relevant technical skills

CORE OBJECTIVES:

* Build industry-relevant technical skills
* Provide practical project experience
* Improve career readiness
* Develop professional skills
* Provide mentorship
* Help learners build project portfolios
* Prepare learners for interviews and employment

TECHNICAL LEARNING:
STEP programs cover different technology and career domains.

Program areas include:

* Data Analytics
* Data Science and AI
* Web Development
* Software Development / SDET
* Cyber Security
* DevOps

PRACTICAL LEARNING:
STEP emphasizes practical application rather than only theoretical learning.

Depending on the program, practical learning can include:

* Mini projects
* Live projects
* Real-world case studies
* Industry-oriented assignments
* Project presentations
* Portfolio development

MENTORSHIP:
STEP includes mentorship and guidance as part of its learning and career-preparation approach.

CAREER PREPARATION:
Career preparation can include:

* Resume development
* LinkedIn profile guidance
* Technical interview preparation
* Mock interviews
* Aptitude and logical reasoning preparation
* Communication and soft-skill development
* HR interview preparation
* Professional development

INDUSTRY EXPOSURE:
STEP emphasizes exposure to practical industry workflows and project-based learning.

Depending on the program, learners may work with:

* Real-world case studies
* Live projects
* Client-style reporting
* Industry-oriented development practices
* Project teams
* Technical mentors

EMPLOYABILITY:
The goal of STEP is to improve employability by combining technical learning, practical experience, professional development, and career preparation.
