SETTRIBE - POLICIES OVERVIEW

PURPOSE:
This section contains general policy information related to SETTribe courses, internships, payments, refunds, participation, certification, privacy, and terms of service.

GENERAL POLICY:
Users and participants are expected to follow the applicable rules and requirements of the course, internship, or program they join.

COURSE POLICIES:
Course-related rules may cover:

* Enrollment
* Attendance
* Learning activities
* Assignments
* Projects
* Assessments
* Certification
* Program participation

INTERNSHIP POLICIES:
Internship-related rules may cover:

* Eligibility
* Application
* Attendance
* Project participation
* Professional conduct
* Project completion
* Evaluation
* Certification

PAYMENT POLICY:
Payment terms depend on the specific course or program. Users should refer to the current official payment information before making a payment.

REFUND POLICY:
Refund eligibility and conditions depend on the applicable SETTribe program and its current refund terms.

CERTIFICATION:
Certification requirements depend on the specific course or internship. A certificate should not be guaranteed unless the applicable official program information confirms the conditions.

ATTENDANCE:
Participants may be expected to maintain the required attendance and participate in scheduled learning, project, or internship activities.

PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT:
Participants are expected to behave professionally and follow applicable organizational and program rules.

PRIVACY:
User information should be handled according to the applicable privacy policy and terms provided by SETTribe.

TERMS AND CONDITIONS:
Users are expected to follow the terms and conditions applicable to the services, programs, courses, or internships they use.

IMPORTANT:
The chatbot must not invent policy conditions.

For questions involving:

* Fees
* Refunds
* Cancellation
* Attendance requirements
* Certification requirements
* Internship rules
* Terms and conditions
* Privacy

the chatbot should use the detailed policy information available in the knowledge base.

If a policy is not available or cannot be verified, the chatbot should clearly state that the information is unavailable and direct the user to SETTribe's official communication channel.

SOURCE:
Official SETTribe policy and program information.
