SETTRIBE - PROJECTS AND WORK AREAS

SETTribe has developed and worked on digital technology solutions across e-Governance, government administration, data systems, dashboards, education, and community-oriented initiatives.

1. E-GOVERNANCE PROJECTS

SETTribe develops digital systems for government and administrative use cases.

Examples include:

* Government dashboards
* Departmental portals
* Administrative monitoring systems
* Workflow management systems
* Field inspection systems
* Data management platforms
* Digital reporting systems

2. MAHAMTS

MahaMTS (Maharashtra Migration Tracking System) is a migration-tracking system developed and maintained by SETTribe.

The system supports tracking and management of migration-related information and beneficiaries.

3. ONE DISTRICT ONE DASHBOARD

SETTribe has worked on One District One Dashboard / Governance Panorama solutions that integrate departmental information into centralized dashboards.

The solution supports areas such as:

* Departmental data integration
* Real-time monitoring
* Data visualization
* GIS-related information
* Geo-tagged field verification
* Executive-level oversight

4. ZILLA PARISHAD PROJECTS

SETTribe has developed technology solutions for Zilla Parishad operations.

Examples include:

* ZP Nagpur Panorama
* ZP Parbhani Field Inspection System
* ZP Parbhani Work Monitoring System
* ZP Hingoli Inter-Department Coordination Platform
* Attendance management systems

These systems support activities such as field inspection, project monitoring, reporting, coordination, dashboards, and administrative management.

5. DISTRICT CONNECT

District Connect is a digital workflow and task-management application associated with district administration.

It supports functions such as:

* Task allocation
* Priority management
* Document handling
* Real-time tracking
* Delay escalation
* Notifications
* Administrative workflows

6. MAHA ASSEMBLY

Maha Assembly is a digital application designed to support communication and coordination during Maharashtra Assembly Sessions among authorized stakeholders.

7. JAL JEEVAN MISSION

SETTribe developed an application for Zilla Parishad Jalna to support tracking of schemes associated with the Jal Jeevan Mission.

8. TRIBAL DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS

SETTribe has worked on technology solutions related to tribal development and community data.

Examples include:

* Dharti Aba
* ITDP Pandharkawada

These applications support data collection, organization, monitoring, and reporting related to tribal development activities.

9. COMMUNITY AND SOCIAL PROJECTS

SETTribe has also developed technology solutions for community-oriented data collection and monitoring.

Examples include projects related to:

* Migration tracking
* Tribal communities
* Rural administration
* Government field operations
* Public-service monitoring

10. DATA AND DASHBOARD PROJECTS

SETTribe develops data-driven systems that can collect information from operational sources and present it through dashboards and reports.

These systems can support:

* Real-time monitoring
* Performance tracking
* Data visualization
* Reporting
* Administrative decision making

11. SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

SETTribe's project work includes:

* Web applications
* Mobile applications
* APIs
* Dashboards
* Database-driven systems
* Workflow systems
* Digital portals

12. EDUCATION AND EMPLOYABILITY

Through STEP by SETTribe, the organization also focuses on skill development and employability.

STEP activities include:

* Technical training
* Practical projects
* Industry-oriented learning
* Mentorship
* Career preparation
* Interview preparation
* Professional development
