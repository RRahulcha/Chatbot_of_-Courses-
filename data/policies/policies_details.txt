SETTRIBE - POLICY DETAILS

1. COURSE POLICY

Course participation is subject to the requirements of the specific course.

Course-related requirements may include:

* Registration
* Attendance
* Assignments
* Practical activities
* Projects
* Assessments
* Program completion

Exact requirements must be taken from the applicable course information.

2. INTERNSHIP POLICY

Internship participants are expected to follow the requirements of their selected internship program.

Internship requirements may include:

* Eligibility verification
* Registration
* Attendance
* Project participation
* Completion of assigned activities
* Professional conduct
* Project submission
* Evaluation

Exact internship rules depend on the specific internship opportunity.

3. PAYMENT POLICY

Payment terms vary according to the selected program.

The chatbot should provide payment information only when it is available in the current official knowledge base.

The chatbot must not:

* Invent fees
* Invent payment deadlines
* Claim a payment is refundable without evidence
* Provide outdated payment information as current

4. REFUND POLICY

Refund conditions depend on the applicable program and official refund policy.

Before providing a refund answer, the chatbot should verify:

* Applicable program
* Current refund terms
* Eligibility conditions
* Applicable timelines
* Any stated exceptions

If the relevant refund information is unavailable, the chatbot should direct the user to the official SETTribe support or inquiry channel.

5. CANCELLATION

Cancellation conditions depend on the applicable course, internship, or service.

The chatbot should provide cancellation information only when supported by the official policy.

6. ATTENDANCE POLICY

Attendance requirements depend on the specific program.

Participants may be expected to attend scheduled:

* Classes
* Training sessions
* Mentoring sessions
* Project activities
* Internship activities
* Assessments

The chatbot should not provide a specific attendance percentage unless it is explicitly available in the applicable official policy.

7. PROJECT POLICY

Where projects are part of a course or internship, participants may be expected to complete assigned project activities according to the program requirements.

Project requirements can include:

* Assignment completion
* Technical implementation
* Documentation
* Submission
* Presentation
* Evaluation

Exact requirements depend on the program.

8. CERTIFICATION POLICY

Certification depends on the requirements of the applicable course or internship.

The chatbot should not automatically promise certification.

If certification is offered, the chatbot should provide the conditions only when they are supported by the relevant official program information.

9. PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT

Participants are expected to maintain professional behavior during courses, internships, projects, mentorship sessions, and other program activities.

Professional conduct includes:

* Respectful communication
* Responsible participation
* Appropriate collaboration
* Following program instructions
* Respecting organizational resources
* Maintaining professional behavior

10. ACADEMIC / PROJECT INTEGRITY

Participants should submit their own work and follow the applicable project and learning requirements.

Any specific rules regarding plagiarism, copying, academic misconduct, or project ownership should only be stated when included in the official policy.

11. PRIVACY

Personal information collected during registration, courses, internships, inquiries, or other services should be handled according to the applicable SETTribe privacy policy.

The chatbot should not make unsupported claims about:

* Data retention periods
* Specific security mechanisms
* Third-party data sharing
* Legal compliance

unless these are explicitly stated in the official privacy policy.

12. TERMS AND CONDITIONS

Use of SETTribe services, programs, websites, courses, and internships may be subject to applicable terms and conditions.

Users should refer to the current official terms and conditions for legally relevant information.

13. POLICY CHANGES

Program rules, fees, schedules, refund conditions, eligibility requirements, and other policies may change.

The chatbot should prefer the latest verified information available in the knowledge base.

14. UNKNOWN POLICY QUESTIONS

If a user asks about a policy that is not present in the knowledge base, the chatbot should not guess.

The appropriate response is to explain that verified information is not available and recommend contacting SETTribe through its official communication channel.

IMPORTANT RAG RULE:

Policy information is authoritative only when supported by the current policy knowledge base.

The chatbot must never:

* Invent refund rules
* Invent fees
* Invent cancellation rules
* Invent attendance percentages
* Guarantee certificates
* Guarantee refunds
* Guarantee placement
* Create legal claims that are not present in the official information

SOURCE:
Official SETTribe policy, program, and terms information.
