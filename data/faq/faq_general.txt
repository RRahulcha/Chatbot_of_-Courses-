What is STEP?

ANSWER:
STEP stands for Skill Transition & Employability Program. It is an initiative by SETTribe focused on technical skills, practical projects, industry-oriented learning, mentorship, and career preparation.

Who is STEP for?

ANSWER:
STEP is designed for students, graduates, early-career learners, and professionals who want to develop industry-relevant skills and prepare for professional careers.

QUESTION:
What is the STEP learning model?

ANSWER:
STEP follows a Learn → Apply → Transition approach. Learners develop skills, apply them through practical projects, and prepare for the transition into professional careers.
